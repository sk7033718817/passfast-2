  • Voice naam me password mat likho
  • Master Password kisi ko mat batao

Privacy Policy

Aakhri update: 8 Oct 2026

Data kahan rehta hai? Aapke passwords, naam aur photo sirf aapke device ke browser storage me, AES-256-GCM encryption ke saath rehte hain. PassFast koi account nahi banata aur koi data apne server par nahi bhejta.

Master Password: ye kahin save nahi hota aur recover nahi ho sakta. Bhool gaye to vault khul nahi sakta.

Cookies: hum cookies nahi use karte. Sirf localStorage (vault aur aapki pasand) use hota hai. Analytics tabhi chalega jab site owner use ON kare aur aap "Haan" dabao; vault ka data kabhi analytics me nahi jaata.

Voice: Chrome jaise browsers bolne ko text banane ke liye audio apne speech service ko bhej sakte hain. Ye browser ka feature hai, PassFast ka nahi.

Aapke adhikaar: "Vault delete" ya browser data clear karke sab kuch mita sakte ho. Sawaal ke liye site owner se sampark karo.
